Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Capsule Kit has no accounts and no sign-up. Your quiz answers, edits and "I already own this" ticks are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so your results are still there when you come back. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data or tapping "Start over" deletes them.

Links to Amazon

When you click a shopping link you leave Capsule Kit and go to Amazon. Amazon may set cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy, including to credit a referral to us if you make a qualifying purchase. We don't receive your name, address or what you bought, only aggregate referral reports.

Images you save

The capsule collage is drawn in your browser. Saving it puts a file on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Fonts and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website host, these services may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads.

Children

Capsule Kit is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions? Contact us here.
