About

A seasonal capsule wardrobe, without the homework.

Capsule Kit turns a quick, tap-through quiz into a complete wardrobe plan for the season ahead. You answer questions about your coloring, your climate, how you spend your days and what you already love wearing. In return you get your color season and palette, about 18 pieces chosen to work together, a week of outfit ideas, and a shopping link for every piece.

How the recommendations work

Your colors come from seasonal color analysis, a method stylists have used for decades to match clothing to undertone, depth and contrast. Fit notes follow common tailoring guidance. Both are a starting point rather than a science: if a color doesn't look right on you in the mirror, trust the mirror.

Each piece is chosen for your season, climate, lifestyle and style, then colored from your palette. Don't like a suggestion? Tap "Not for me" to swap the piece, change its color, adjust the fit or fabric, or remove it.

About the shopping links

Every link opens a live Amazon search for that piece in your color and price range, described by the cut and details of this season's popular versions. Results change as listings do, so browse a few options before you buy. Price ranges shown in the app are our estimates, not Amazon's prices.

Capsule Kit may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through these links, at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend.
